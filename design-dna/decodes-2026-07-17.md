# Decodes — 2026-07-17 (the grounds day)

The day the homepage's visual system settled. Three reference decodes
(Apple ground rotation, Hormozi fold, viral-sma constraint) plus the
laws they produced. Written same-day; commits cited.

## 1 · THE STAGE STAYS QUIET (the day's master law)

Stages wear neither **radii** nor **drama**.

- **Hard cuts between sections.** Rounding belongs to objects ON the
  stage (cards, capsules, trays). A cut reads as a scene change; a
  rounded seam reads as furniture. (abb1946)
- **Two grounds only, used SPARINGLY.** White + #f5f5f7, switched at
  chapter breaks — mostly ONE ground with an occasional gray chapter,
  never strict alternation. The zebra (a flip every band) lived one
  hour: bands ≈ 1 viewport + a flip per band = a seam always on
  screen; the page never settles. Long same-ground runs separate via
  full-viewport bands, strong heads, and per-section object families.
  (2444c5e → ee10ed1)
- **Dark lives in OBJECTS** (receipt cards, report card, bezels) —
  never as a mid-page band on a light page (the ink proof stage lived
  one day, ea712d5 → 4f5619b). Full-dark stages belong to all-dark
  cinematic pages only (iPhone-Pro style). The steel closer is the
  page's ONE colored ground — a finale exception.
- **Object rule (both directions):** gray objects on white stages
  (audit tray, witness quotes), white shadow-cards on gray stages
  (service cards' recipe). The tray/gray-field = "grouped region";
  the give's field is distinguished by holding an input.
- **Why Apple pages differ:** ground = the stage the imagery needs.
  Dark hardware needs a black stage (Pro pages, end to end — one
  film, no switches). Catalog/hub pages rotate the two lights at
  errand boundaries. Switch at narrative chapter breaks only when the
  incoming content demands a different stage — never for variety.
  Hubs earn switches; single-story pages earn 0–1.

## 2 · THE MOTION TAXONOMY (why "simple" wins — the real rule)

"Simple" is the wrong word for Google/Apple/SearchKings — the word is
**quiet**: heavily designed to not be noticed. Attention is the page's
only currency; every animation spends some. Sort all motion:

| Type | What it does | Verdict |
|---|---|---|
| Demonstrating | shows the product working (search-cycle, audit skeleton, their autoplay reels) | always earns its place — proof, not decoration |
| Responding | reacts to the visitor (hovers, pill slide, odometers on arrival) | small doses — rewards attention, never demands it |
| Ambient | one quiet living element (the roll, the marquee) | 1–2 per page so it isn't dead |
| Performing | scroll-jacks, morphs, spectacle | only when spectacle IS the product (Rhythm Influence selling culture); for selling bookings: never |

Rhythm Influence verdict (measured: 4200px pinned scroll-jack, 6
portrait autoplay videos, cards dimmed to 0.3, no per-card CTA, ~30
video requests on load): a mood reel, correct FOR THEM because reels
ARE their product (their realism law), wrong for a router. Stealable:
the dim-all-but-one focus mechanic; the portrait-reel card as a
future container for real client video (Work page / proof, when
footage exists).

## 3 · VIRAL-SMA DECODE (why total constraint reads as design)

Measured: warm cream two-tone (#efebe5 ground / #f7f2ea cards), ONE
font weight (500) nearly everywhere, ZERO accent color, ZERO shadows
(depth = the two-tone alternation alone), ONE spacing value (80px
every section), ONE signature interaction (text-slide on every pill
CTA). H1 100px/500/-1.5px. No pricing anywhere — pure book-a-call
funnel; safety = "month-to-month, cancel anytime" said twice.

**The lesson: what reads as "really designed" is the absence of
decisions the eye must reconcile.** Constraint = coherence. All of
Jake's references (SearchKings, Hormozi, Apple, viral-sma) share one
spine — disciplined attention systems — across different palettes.

**Their case-study pattern (Jake likes; fold into our proof):**
- Eyebrow "Client results" + NARRATIVE title — a verb-led story
  ("Scaling a beauty brand with reels"), not the client name.
- Exactly TWO big stats per case, count-up on entry, each with a
  time qualifier ("In the first 30 days") — the qualifier is the
  credibility. Fewer numbers, harder landing.
- Split layout, mirrored on alternate case studies; portrait media.
- Ours keeps the dark receipt card (house grammar: dark object on
  gray) but adopts the editorial interior: narrative title, stat
  count discipline, time qualifiers. Mirror-alternation activates
  when client #2 exists.

**Adopted same-day:** count-up → upgraded to B12 mechanical odometers
on the proof receipts (62bc756; the library's carry-window +
power-ease + integer-snap recipe).

**Queued (Phase 3, copy-led):** the comparison table ("Other
agencies vs us" — checks/crosses, our rows: lock-in/pricing/AI
answer speed/ownership/tracking) and the MISSION BAND — a 2–3 line
concrete value-reframe (mini-about: identity + philosophy + Mesa
locality), placed between Services and Proof, in the statement
register; never an abstract manifesto. The orphaned value-reframe
component may be its vehicle.
